FluidR3 General MIDI samples

Original Fluid soundfont by Frank Wen and contributors.
FluidR3_GM.sf2 is distributed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/

MP3 rendering and distribution: Michael Gleitz and contributors.
Percussion-inclusive distribution maintained by Paul Rosen:
https://github.com/paulrosen/midi-js-soundfonts
https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts

This application bundles distortion guitar, overdriven guitar, clean electric
 guitar, fingered electric bass, picked electric bass, and percussion samples.
The original base64 MP3 data is unchanged. The JavaScript wrappers were removed
and note-name keys converted to MIDI numbers in declarative JSON files.
